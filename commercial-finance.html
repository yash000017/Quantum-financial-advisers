Commercial Finance

Commercial Finance for UK Businesses

Home / Commercial Finance

Access to appropriate funding can be an important part of purchasing property, developing a site, expanding a business or restructuring existing commercial borrowing.

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd works with UK companies and corporate borrowers seeking funding for genuine business and commercial purposes.

Our role is to understand the funding requirement, help prepare relevant information and identify potential commercial funding routes appropriate to the transaction.

Commercial finance requirements may include:

  • Purchase of commercial premises
  • Refinancing business property
  • Property development
  • Short-term commercial bridging
  • Limited company property investment
  • Business expansion
  • Refinancing existing commercial borrowing
  • Corporate property acquisition

Our process

We start with an initial assessment covering:

  • Borrower: who is borrowing?
  • Purpose: what will the finance be used for?
  • Property or Security: what assets are involved?
  • Occupancy: who occupies or will occupy the property?
  • Funding Requirement: how much funding is required?
  • Exit Strategy: where applicable, how will short-term borrowing be repaid?
  • Regulatory Perimeter: does the proposed transaction involve an FCA-regulated activity?

Only once we are satisfied that the proposed service is within the scope of activities we are able to undertake will we progress the enquiry.

Important information

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd does not provide regulated mortgage advice, consumer credit advice, investment advice, pension advice or insurance advice.

Regulatory Status

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the FCA and is not an Appointed Representative; we do not advise on or arrange FCA-regulated financial products. We work with a panel of lenders, whose details are available on request, to identify potentially suitable commercial finance options.

We receive commission from lenders, with amounts varying by lender and commission model; our commission model is based on a percentage of the amount borrowed, with the calculation and amount disclosed throughout your customer journey.

Commercial finance and other services are subject to lender approval, underwriting and individual lender criteria. Security and personal guarantees may be required. Failure to maintain repayments may result in enforcement action against assets provided as security.
