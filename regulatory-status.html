Regulatory Status

Status of Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd

Home / Regulatory Status

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and is not an Appointed Representative of an FCA-authorised firm.

The company therefore does not undertake activities requiring FCA authorisation unless a specific statutory exclusion or exemption is applicable and has been confirmed as part of our regulatory-perimeter assessment.

Services we do not provide

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd does not provide advice on or arrange:

  • Regulated residential mortgages
  • First-time buyer residential mortgages
  • Residential remortgages
  • Regulated residential bridging finance
  • Consumer buy-to-let business
  • Equity release
  • Lifetime mortgages
  • Life insurance
  • Critical illness insurance
  • Income protection
  • Private medical insurance
  • Buildings or contents insurance
  • Investments
  • Savings products constituting regulated investments
  • Pension products or pension transfers
  • Other regulated financial products

Commercial finance

Our financial services are restricted to commercial finance and business funding activities that fall outside the FCA regulatory perimeter.

Before accepting an engagement, we may obtain information concerning the identity and legal status of the borrower, purpose of borrowing, property or assets involved, proposed occupation of property, security and other relevant circumstances.

This enables us to establish whether the proposed transaction is within the scope of services we are able to provide.

Where we consider that a proposed transaction may involve a regulated activity, we may decline to act.

No regulated financial advice

Information on this website is general information concerning commercial finance.

Nothing on this website constitutes a personal recommendation or regulated financial advice concerning investments, pensions, insurance, regulated mortgages or other FCA-regulated products.

FCA-authorised advice

If you require advice regarding an FCA-regulated financial product, you should obtain advice from a firm appropriately authorised for the relevant activity.

You can check whether a financial-services firm is authorised through the FCA's Financial Services Register.

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the FCA and is not an Appointed Representative; we do not advise on or arrange FCA-regulated financial products. We work with a panel of lenders, whose details are available on request, to identify potentially suitable commercial finance options.

We receive commission from lenders, with amounts varying by lender and commission model; our commission model is based on a percentage of the amount borrowed, with the calculation and amount disclosed throughout your customer journey.

Commercial finance and other services are subject to lender approval, underwriting and individual lender criteria. Security and personal guarantees may be required. Failure to maintain repayments may result in enforcement action against assets provided as security.
