Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few of the questions we hear most often. Can't find yours? Just ask.

Is the initial conversation really free?

Yes. Our first conversation is always free and comes with no obligation — it's simply a chance for us to understand what you need and explain how we might help.

Do you only work with clients in London?

We work with clients across the UK. Most conversations can happen by phone or video call, whatever suits you best.

What's the difference between a will and a trust?

A will sets out what should happen to your estate after you pass away. A trust is a separate legal arrangement that can hold assets on behalf of others, often used alongside a will as part of wider estate planning.
