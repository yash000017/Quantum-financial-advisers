Helping UK businesses, property companies and corporate investors access funding for commercial and investment purposes.

Who we work with

Commercial funding built around your business

We work primarily with:

  • UK limited companies
  • Property investment companies
  • Corporate property SPVs
  • Property developers
  • Trading businesses
  • Commercial property investors
  • Companies purchasing their own business premises
  • Companies refinancing existing commercial borrowing

Every enquiry is initially reviewed to establish the nature of the borrower, property, security and purpose of the funding.

Where a proposed transaction may constitute regulated mortgage business, consumer credit business or another FCA-regulated activity, Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd will not provide regulated advice or arrange that transaction.

Our approach

A practical approach to commercial finance

1

Understand

We begin by understanding your business, the proposed transaction, the amount required, available security, timescale and intended repayment strategy.

2

Assess

We assess whether the proposed transaction falls within the scope of the commercial finance services we are able to provide.

3

Prepare

We help organise the information normally required by commercial lenders, which may include accounts, management information, cash-flow forecasts, property details, development appraisals and supporting documentation.

4

Explore

Where appropriate, we identify potential commercial lenders or funding routes willing to consider the transaction.

5

Progress

We help coordinate information requests and communication during the commercial funding process.

Important regulatory information

Our regulatory position

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and is not an Appointed Representative.

Our activities are restricted to commercial finance and other services which fall outside the FCA regulatory perimeter.

We do not provide advice on or arrange:

  • Residential mortgages
  • First-time buyer mortgages
  • Residential remortgages
  • Consumer buy-to-let
  • Equity release
  • Life insurance
  • Critical illness insurance
  • Income protection
  • Private medical insurance
  • Home insurance
  • Investments
  • Pensions

If you require advice concerning a regulated financial product, you should seek assistance from an appropriately FCA-authorised firm.
