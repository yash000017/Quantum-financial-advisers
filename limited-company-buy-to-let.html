Limited Company Buy-to-Let

Property Investment Finance for Corporate Borrowers

Home › Limited Company Buy-to-Let

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd considers buy-to-let funding enquiries from UK limited companies and corporate property SPVs purchasing or refinancing residential property for genuine commercial investment purposes.

Who this service is for

Our service is primarily intended for:

  • Limited company landlords
  • Property investment companies
  • Corporate SPVs
  • Corporate portfolio landlords
  • Companies acquiring residential investment property

The property must be intended for commercial letting to tenants and not for occupation as the home of the borrower, directors or their family.

Purchase

We may assist corporate borrowers seeking finance to acquire property for commercial letting.

Refinance

We may also consider corporate refinancing enquiries relating to existing investment property portfolios.

Information typically required

This may include:

  • Company details
  • Property details
  • Expected or existing rent
  • Deposit or equity
  • Directors' property experience
  • Existing property portfolio
  • Company accounts where applicable
  • Source of deposit
  • Intended ownership structure

Regulatory restriction

We do not undertake consumer buy-to-let business and do not advise on or arrange regulated residential mortgages.

All enquiries are subject to an initial regulatory-perimeter assessment before we determine whether we can assist.

Regulatory Status

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and is not an Appointed Representative of an FCA-authorised firm. We do not provide advice on or arrange regulated residential mortgages, consumer buy-to-let business, equity release, investments, pensions, life insurance, protection insurance, general insurance or other FCA-regulated financial products.

Our services are limited to commercial finance and other activities which, following an initial regulatory-perimeter assessment, fall outside the FCA regulatory perimeter. Where an enquiry relates to a regulated financial product or regulated activity, we will not provide advice on or arrange that transaction.

Information on this website is provided for general information only and does not constitute advice or a personal recommendation in relation to any FCA-regulated financial product.

Commercial finance is subject to lender approval, underwriting and individual lender criteria. Security and personal guarantees may be required. Failure to maintain repayments may result in enforcement action against assets provided as security.
