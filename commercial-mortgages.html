Commercial Mortgages

Commercial Mortgages

Finance for Business and Commercial Property

Home / Commercial Mortgages

A commercial mortgage can be used by a business to purchase or refinance property used for genuine commercial purposes.

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd works with corporate borrowers seeking finance in relation to commercial property transactions.

Examples may include:

  • Offices
  • Retail premises
  • Warehouses
  • Industrial units
  • Business premises
  • Commercial investment property
  • Hotels and hospitality premises
  • Care sector premises
  • Nurseries and educational premises
  • Other commercial property

Owner-occupied commercial property

A company may wish to purchase premises from which it operates rather than continuing to rent.

We can help understand the proposed transaction, funding requirement, deposit or equity available and the financial information likely to be required by commercial lenders.

Commercial investment property

We can also consider enquiries from corporate investors purchasing or refinancing property held for commercial investment purposes.

What commercial lenders may consider

The information required will depend on the transaction and lender but may include:

  • Business accounts
  • Management accounts
  • Bank statements
  • Business plans
  • Property valuation
  • Lease information
  • Rental income
  • Deposit or equity available
  • Existing borrowing
  • Directors' experience
  • Proposed repayment strategy

Regulatory scope

Before progressing an enquiry, we consider the borrower, proposed use of the property and security offered.

We do not advise on or arrange mortgages relating to an individual's home or other regulated residential mortgage transactions.

Regulatory Status

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the FCA and is not an Appointed Representative; we do not advise on or arrange FCA-regulated financial products. We work with a panel of lenders, whose details are available on request, to identify potentially suitable commercial finance options.

We receive commission from lenders, with amounts varying by lender and commission model; our commission model is based on a percentage of the amount borrowed, with the calculation and amount disclosed throughout your customer journey.

Commercial finance and other services are subject to lender approval, underwriting and individual lender criteria. Security and personal guarantees may be required. Failure to maintain repayments may result in enforcement action against assets provided as security.
