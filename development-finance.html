Development Finance

Property Development Finance

Funding Support for Corporate Property Development Projects

Home / Development Finance

Property development finance is commonly used by developers and property companies to fund the acquisition and development of property.

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd works with UK limited companies and corporate SPVs undertaking development projects for business or investment purposes.

Projects we may be able to assist with

  • Ground-up property development
  • Residential development undertaken by corporate developers
  • Commercial development
  • Property conversion
  • Heavy refurbishment
  • Site acquisition and development
  • Conversion of commercial property
  • Existing development refinancing
  • Development exit funding

Understanding the project

Development lenders commonly consider factors including:

  • Purchase price
  • Current property or site value
  • Development costs
  • Gross development value
  • Borrower's contribution
  • Development experience
  • Planning permission
  • Build programme
  • Professional team
  • Proposed exit strategy

Preparing a funding proposal

We can help organise and present the information relevant to a commercial development funding application. This may include:

  • Development appraisal
  • Schedule of works
  • Planning documentation
  • Cost information
  • Professional reports
  • Company information
  • Borrower experience
  • Financial accounts
  • Exit strategy

Exit strategy

The proposed repayment strategy is an important consideration for short-term development finance.

Common commercial exit strategies may include sale of the completed development or refinancing onto an appropriate longer-term commercial facility.

Regulatory scope

Our development finance services are available only where the proposed transaction falls outside the FCA regulatory perimeter.

Regulatory Status

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the FCA and is not an Appointed Representative; we do not advise on or arrange FCA-regulated financial products. We work with a panel of lenders, whose details are available on request, to identify potentially suitable commercial finance options.

We receive commission from lenders, with amounts varying by lender and commission model; our commission model is based on a percentage of the amount borrowed, with the calculation and amount disclosed throughout your customer journey.

Commercial finance and other services are subject to lender approval, underwriting and individual lender criteria. Security and personal guarantees may be required. Failure to maintain repayments may result in enforcement action against assets provided as security.
