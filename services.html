Wills & Trust

A clear, up-to-date will is one of the simplest things you can do for the people you love — yet many people put it off. We help you put a valid will in place, written in plain English, that reflects exactly what you want to happen.

We also guide you through setting up trusts, which can be a useful way to hold and protect assets for family members, including children or vulnerable relatives.

  • Straightforward wills, written in plain English
  • Guidance on setting up family trusts
  • Regular reviews as your circumstances change

Estate Planning

Estate planning is about more than a single document — it's the bigger picture of how your affairs are arranged, so your wishes are clear and your family faces as little complication as possible.

We take the time to understand your family circumstances and talk through the options available, always explained clearly and without pressure.

  • General planning guidance around your estate
  • Support thinking through family circumstances
  • Plans reviewed as life changes
