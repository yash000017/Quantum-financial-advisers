Commercial Bridging Finance

Short-Term Funding for Commercial and Corporate Transactions

Home › Commercial Bridging

Commercial bridging finance is short-term funding that may be used where a company requires finance for a time-sensitive business or investment transaction.

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd considers commercial bridging enquiries from corporate borrowers where the proposed activity falls outside the FCA regulatory perimeter.

Commercial bridging may be used for

  • Commercial property acquisition
  • Auction purchases by corporate borrowers
  • Short-term refinancing
  • Property investment transactions
  • Funding pending sale of another commercial asset
  • Refurbishment projects
  • Development exit
  • Time-sensitive corporate property transactions

Understanding the exit

Because bridging finance is normally short-term, lenders usually require a clearly identified repayment or exit strategy. This could include:

  • Sale of an investment property
  • Refinancing onto longer-term commercial borrowing
  • Receipt of another identifiable source of business funds

Important restriction

Quantum Financial Advisers Ltd does not provide advice on or arrange regulated residential bridging finance.

In particular, we do not assist with bridging finance to purchase or refinance a home occupied or intended to be occupied by the borrower or their family where the transaction constitutes regulated mortgage business.
